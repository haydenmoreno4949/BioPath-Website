What Is Biotechnology?

Definition:

Biotechnology is the use of biology and living organisms to create products, tools, and technologies that improve human life and health of the planet. Biotechnology is used in fields like medicine, agriculture, industrial and environment, and diagnostic research.

What is the difference between biotechnology companies and pharmaceutical companies? :

While Biotechnology and Pharmaceutical Companies do have lots of similarities and overlap in what they do, biotechnology specifically uses living organisms to produce the products they market while pharmaceutical companies use not only living organisms, but also things like synthetic molecules, inorganic reagents and solvents, and purified enzymes. Some companies are considered Biopharmaceutical which is a mix between Biotechnology and Pharmaceutical where they combine biological science and chemical manufacturing to develop, scale, and commercialize large molecule drugs derived from living organisms.
