Navigating Life Sciences

What Is Biotechnology?

Biotechnology is the use of biology and living organisms to create products, tools, and technologies that improve human life and the health of the planet.

Biotechnology is used in fields like:

  • Medicine
  • Agriculture
  • Industrial and environment
  • Diagnostic research

What is the difference between biotechnology companies and pharmaceutical companies?

Biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies have a lot of similarities and overlap in what they do. The key difference is what goes into the products they market.

Biotechnology

Biotechnology companies use living organisms to produce the products they market.

Living organisms only

Biopharmaceutical

Some companies are biopharmaceutical, a mix between biotechnology and pharmaceutical. They combine biological science and chemical manufacturing to develop, scale, and commercialize large molecule drugs derived from living organisms.

A mix of both

Pharmaceutical

Pharmaceutical companies use not only living organisms, but also things like synthetic molecules, inorganic reagents and solvents, and purified enzymes.

Living organisms and other materials
