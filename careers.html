Quality Control

3 Roles
Quality Control QC-01

Quality Control & Assurance

Performs testing related to the manufacturing of cell therapy products for clinical trials and commercial operations in a controlled cGMP cleanroom environment.

Education Needed

Bachelor's Degree in Science, Engineering, or equivalent technical discipline.

Salary (New Jersey)

Entry: $54,200/yr | Median: $71,252/yr | Senior: $80,400/yr

Key Responsibilities:
  • Conduct analytical testing of raw materials/utilities, in-process, or final autologous CAR-T products.
  • Ensure compliance with procedures, safety policies, and cGMP regulations.
  • Write peer reviews and approve laboratory data using Electronic Laboratory Systems (LIMS).
  • Work routinely in cleanrooms with biological materials and hazardous chemicals.
Day-to-Day Life:

Varied daily routine depending on machine calibration or processing issues. Activities involve document updates, reference compliance checks (QSR), and laboratory testing cycles.

Quality Control QC-02

Quality Engineer

Creates processes, testing procedures, and system implementations to ensure biological and industrial products fulfill strict quality and safety regulations.

Education Needed

Bachelor's in Engineering with experience. Preferred: Master's degree & advanced certifications.

Salary

Entry: $78,700–$85,319 | Median: $102,597/yr | Senior: $126,800–$152,128

Day-to-Day Life:

Develop, test, revise, and audit manufacturing processes continuously through both internal company audits and external supplier location audits. Quality engineers advise product developers on modified plans, cost impact, and project timeline feasibility.

Quality Control QC-03

Quality Control Technician

Inspects and tests raw materials, components, and finished products in manufacturing environments to maintain product consistency and compliance.

Education Progression

High School Diploma → Associate Degree/Certificates → Hands-on Experience → Certified Quality Technician (CQT).

Salary

Entry: $38,000/yr | Median: $48,000/yr | Senior: $75,000/yr
*Pharma technicians often earn higher wages.

Technician Specializations:
Manufacturing Material Product Statistical
Day-to-Day Life:

Uses specialized testing equipment to inspect incoming materials, resolves quality issues on the shop floor, maintains strict testing logs, and works directly with production teams on corrective actions.

Clinical Research

3 Roles
Clinical Research CR-01

Clinical Research & Regulatory Affairs

Protects patient safety by managing human clinical trials and serving as the primary liaison navigating drugs through FDA approval onto the commercial market.

Education Needed

Bachelor's in Life Sciences + 1,500 to 3,000 hours in human subjects research. Advanced knowledge of GCP & HIPAA required.

Salary

Entry: $49,700/yr | Median: $108,971/yr | Senior: $192,400/yr

Focus Areas:

Regulatory Affairs (RA): Oversees FDA reviews and drug submissions.
Clinical Research: Direct patient trial execution and health outcome tracking.

FDA Regulatory Science Guide

Day-to-Day Life:

Mornings involve study recruitment, patient scheduling, and progress meetings. Afternoons shift to internal quality audits, electronic data capture, protocol reviews, and evening scientific journal reading.

Clinical Research CR-02

Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC)

Works directly under the Principal Investigator (PI) to manage daily clinical trial operations, patient visits, compliance, and study device/medication tracking.

Education Needed

Bachelor's in science or medical field (nursing, public health, medical tech) + 2 years hands-on experience or internships.

Salary

Entry: $45,000–$55,000 | Median: $60,000–$70,000 | Senior: $75,000–$90,000

Day-to-Day Life:

CRCs arrive early to prep study kits, review appointment schedules, dispense trial medications according to strict protocol, coordinate with patients, and log data for study sponsors.

Clinical Research CR-03

Pharmacovigilance Specialist

Monitors and reports the safety profiles of drugs and biologics by investigating Individual Case Study Reports (ICSRs) and adverse drug reactions (ADRs).

Education Needed

Bachelor's degree in healthcare/science field. Advanced roles prefer MD, MS, or PhD.

Salary

Entry: $53,991–$65,120 | Median: $96,145–$123,670 | Senior: $135,647–$173,138

Day-to-Day Life:

Evaluates safety databases, reviews clinical trial reports, logs patient adverse events, determines if reactions are expected vs. unexpected, and files regulatory safety submissions with global authorities like the FDA or EMA.

Research & Development

3 Roles
R&D Discovery RD-01

Preclinical Discovery Research

Identifies, tests, and optimizes new drug candidates using cellular models and laboratory assays to evaluate efficacy and toxicity before human testing.

Degree Needed

Entry: B.S. in Biology, Biochemistry, or Biotechnology.
Advanced: M.S. (3-5 yrs lab exp) or Ph.D. in Pharmacology.

Salary

Entry: $60,000–$83,000 | Median: $103,000–$125,000 | Senior: $150,000–$180,000+

Day-to-Day Life:

Spends full days in wet labs performing PCR assays, maintaining cell cultures, administering test doses in disease models, processing raw experimental data with analytical software, and keeping FDA-compliant lab notebooks.

R&D Discovery RD-02

Bioinformatics

Combines computer science, statistics, and genomics to build computational pipelines, analyze genetic sequence data, and discover therapeutic targets.

Degree Needed

Entry: B.S. in Bioinformatics, Computer Science, or Data Science.
Senior: M.S. or Ph.D. with programming expertise.

Salary

Entry: $70,000–$90,000 | Median: $105,000–$130,000 | Senior: $155,000–$195,000+

Day-to-Day Life:

Writes code (Python/R) to parse biological datasets, models drug-body interactions, queries genomic databases, and meets with wet-lab scientists to translate computational findings into research next steps.

R&D Discovery RD-03

Lab Safety Professional

Oversees health, regulatory, and environmental safety across research labs to ensure teams handle chemical and biological samples safely.

Degree Needed

Entry: B.S. in Environmental Health & Safety, Occupational Health, Biology, or Chemistry.
Senior: M.S. in Industrial Hygiene or Public Health.

Salary

Entry: $55,000–$75,000 | Median: $85,000–$105,000 | Senior: $120,000–$165,000+

Day-to-Day Life:

Inspects research equipment and biosafety cabinets, conducts chemical risk assessments for new experiments, leads safety orientation for new scientists, and responds to facility incidents.

Operations

2 Roles
Operations OP-01

Process / Product Development

Translates early laboratory bench concepts into scalable, cost-effective, and reproducible commercial manufacturing methods for therapeutics.

Degree Needed

Degree in Biotechnology, Bioprocess Engineering, Molecular Biology, or Chemistry.

Salary

Entry: $90,000–$115,000 | Median: $92,300–$106,700 | Senior: $120,000–$160,000+

Day-to-Day Life:

Optimizes fermentation conditions, cell growth yields, and purification steps to scale lab biological drug discovery into high-volume manufacturing lines.

Operations OP-02

Manufacturing / Production

Operates specialized bioprocessing equipment like bioreactors and filtration units in regulated cleanrooms to produce vaccines and medicines.

Degree Needed

Bachelor's in Science/Engineering. *Entry-level roles may accept an Associate Degree or High School Diploma.

Salary

Entry: $45,000–$60,000 | Median: $65,000–$85,000 | Senior: $110,000–$150,000+

Day-to-Day Life:

Works inside cleanrooms adhering to Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and cGMP standards, cleans and sterilizes bioreactor vessels, dispenses biological raw materials, and coordinates with quality assurance.

Finance & Administration

4 Roles
Finance & Admin FA-01

Administration

Coordinates daily corporate operations, scheduling, research budgets, lab supply procurement, and compliance documentation for biotech organizations.

Degree Needed

High School Diploma/GED required. Minimum Bachelor's in Business, Administration, or Life Sciences preferred.

Salary

Entry: $48,562/yr | Median: $72,585/yr | Senior: $108,491/yr

Day-to-Day Life:

Reviews executive and scientist schedules, processes specialized supply orders, coordinates travel itineraries for scientific conferences, and manages compliance documentation for auditing visits.

Finance & Admin FA-02

Biotech Legal Counsel

Manages intellectual property (IP), drafts patent strategies, negotiates corporate technology licensing, and navigates FDA legal liabilities.

Degree Needed

Juris Doctor (JD) Degree + State Bar Admission with expertise in biotechnology IP and contract law.

Salary

Entry: $33,000/yr | Median: $57,731/yr | Senior: $89,000/yr

Day-to-Day Life:

Advises business units on legal risk, researches upcoming legislative policies, manages lease agreements and patent databases, and reviews licensing agreements for scientific commercialization.

Finance & Admin FA-03

Biotech Finance

Manages financial capital, R&D burn rates, clinical trial budgeting, venture funding grants, and accounting operations across drug pipelines.

Degree Needed

Bachelor's in Finance, Accounting, Business, or Economics. Minor in biology/biotech is advantageous; Master's preferred for senior roles.

Salary

Entry: $55,000–$75,000 | Median: $75,000–$105,000 | Senior: $105,000–$135,000+

Day-to-Day Life:

Tracks fast-moving R&D cash burn rates, reviews vendor invoices, collaborates with scientific project leads to update project budgets, and evaluates capital allocation for lab equipment.

Finance & Admin FA-04

Business Development

Drives corporate growth through strategic industry partnerships, technology licensing deals, mergers, and investor relations.

Degree Needed

Bachelor's in Life Sciences or Business/Finance. Hybrid background favored; MBA or Ph.D. preferred for senior roles.

Salary

Entry: $85,000–$115,000 | Median: $125,000–$165,000 | Senior: $175,000–$240,000+

Day-to-Day Life:

Evaluates clinical trial data with R&D teams, constructs financial valuation models for drug assets, pitches to pharma partners, negotiates licensing deal terms, and manages investor relationships.
