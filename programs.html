NJ Extracurricular Summer Programs

Intensive summer fellowships, laboratory bootcamps, and pre-college programs across New Jersey designed for high school students.

Summer Fellowship 01

Biotechnology Research Fellowship

  • Caldwell University — Caldwell, NJ
  • Duration: 3 Weeks
  • Fee (2026): $2,300 ($1,800 early bird before May 1st)

A 3-week intensive summer program that lets high school students perform hands-on research in cancer biology, neuroscience, and microbiology.

Visit website
Free Program 02

Pathways to Partners in Science

  • Liberty Science Center — Jersey City, NJ
  • Target Grade: Rising 10th Graders
  • Cost: Free for accepted participants

A 2-week intensive summer experience for rising 10th grade students featuring hands-on workshops, mini projects, and a final capstone presentation.

Visit website
Free + Stipend 03

Partners in Science

  • Liberty Science Center — Jersey City, NJ
  • Target Grade: Rising 11th & 12th Graders
  • Cost: Free attendance + completion stipend

An 8-week intensive summer experience paired with mentors in science, health, and technical fields to perform authentic research.

Visit website
Lab Science Camp 04

DNA Learning Center Science Camps

  • Passaic County Biotech Innovation Center — Wayne, NJ
  • Audience: Middle & High School Students
  • Fee (2026): $600 to $700 per participant

Learn advanced lab and computer equipment for real-world biotech applications like DNA extraction, genetic engineering, and molecular biology.

Visit website
GenBank Publishing 05

Waksman Institute Summer Experience

  • Rutgers University — Piscataway, NJ
  • In-Person (2026): $2,200 (2 Weeks)
  • Virtual (2026): $600 (2 Weeks)

Daily seminars and lab work to purify/analyze DNA fragments and publish gene sequences on GenBank. Features a virtual non-lab option.

Visit website

College Credit 07

Summer Scholars Program

  • Rutgers University — New Brunswick, NJ
  • Requirements: Age 16+ by course start
  • Fees (2026): $162 fee + tuition per credit

Allows qualified high school students to take up to two college courses worth 3 credits each to immerse themselves in university academics.

Visit website
Toxicology & Health 08

Toxicology, Health & Environmental Disease

  • Rutgers University — Piscataway, NJ
  • Prerequisite: 1+ High School Biology course
  • Fee (2026): $973 per student

Offers rising 10th–12th graders hands-on experience in lab ethics, experimental design, genetics, pharmacogenetics, and clinical toxicology.
