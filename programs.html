NJ Extracurricular Summer Programs

Summer Fellowship 01

Biotechnology Research Fellowship

Caldwell University — Caldwell, NJ

A 3-week intensive summer program that lets high school students perform hands-on research in cancer biology, neuroscience, and microbiology.

Duration: 3 Weeks

Fee (2026): $2,300 per participant by May 15th ($1,800 early bird before May 1st)

Research & Cancer Biology Visit Website
Free Program 02

Pathways to Partners in Science

Liberty Science Center — Jersey City, NJ

A 2-week intensive summer experience for rising 10th grade students featuring hands-on workshops, mini projects, and a final capstone presentation.

Target Grade: Rising 10th Graders

Cost: Free for accepted participants

Workshops & Capstone Visit Website
Free + Stipend 03

Partners in Science

Liberty Science Center — Jersey City, NJ

An 8-week intensive summer experience for rising 11th and 12th graders paired with mentors in science, health, and technical fields to perform authentic research.

Target Grade: Rising 11th & 12th Graders

Cost: Free attendance + completion stipend

Mentorship & Authentic Research Visit Website
Lab Science Camp 04

DNA Learning Center Science Camps

Passaic County Biotech Innovation Center — Wayne, NJ

Designed for middle and high school students to learn advanced lab and computer equipment for real-world biotech applications like DNA extraction, genetic engineering, and molecular biology.

Audience: Middle & High School Students

Fee (2026): $600 to $700 per participant

Genetic Engineering & DNA Visit Website
GenBank Publishing 05

Waksman Institute Summer Experience (WISE)

Rutgers University (Busch Campus) — Piscataway, NJ

A 2-week non-residential program featuring daily seminars and lab work to purify/analyze DNA fragments and publish gene sequences on GenBank. Also features a virtual non-lab option.

In-Person (2026): $2,200 (2 weeks post-approval, no refunds)

Virtual Option (2026): $600 (No lab work; 2 weeks post-approval)

Molecular Biology & Bioinformatics Visit Website
Pre-College Portal 06

Rutgers Pre-College Programs (Grades 9-12)

Rutgers University — Statewide Portal

Rutgers offers a diverse directory of pre-college academic programs to enhance awareness of the college experience, provide extracurriculars, and increase college preparedness.

Eligibility: High School Students (Grades 9–12)

Format: Multiple academic & STEM discipline options

College Readiness Visit Website
College Credit 07

Summer Scholars Program

Rutgers University — New Brunswick / Piscataway, NJ

Allows high schoolers (min age 16) to take up to two program-approved college courses worth 3 credits each at Rutgers-New Brunswick to immerse themselves in college academics.

Requirements: Age 16+ by course start (non-graduated)

Fees (2026): $162 student fee + $482–$1,162 per credit

College Coursework Visit Website
Toxicology & Health 08

Toxicology, Health, and Environmental Disease (THED)

Rutgers University — Piscataway, NJ

Offers rising 10th–12th graders hands-on experience in lab ethics, experimental design, genetics, pharmacogenetics, clinical toxicology, and career pathways across medicine and research.

Prerequisite: Completed 1+ High School Biology course

Fee (2026): $973 per student

Toxicology & Biomedical Visit Website

Volunteering Opportunities

Note on Requirements: Please keep in mind that some volunteer positions have specific age limits (such as 16+ or 18+), require background checks, or expect prior education/interest in the life sciences.
K-12 Teaching Outreach 01

Rutgers BIO Links Outreach Program

New Brunswick Middle & High School

Volunteers are paired with a local science teacher to visit one classroom per week over nine weeks, serving as an opportunity to build presentation and collaboration skills.

Commitment: 1 Visit/Week for 9 Weeks

Focus: Science Communication & Teaching

Educational Outreach Visit Website
STEM Mentorship 02

Students 2 Science (S2S) Volunteering

Students 2 Science — Newark & Whippany, NJ

Volunteers guide students through hands-on laboratory activities focusing on core biotech concepts. No teaching experience required, but must have interest in life sciences.

Age Requirement: Age 18 and older

Requirement: Interest/experience in biotech or life sciences

Laboratory Mentorship Visit Website
University Outreach 03

Science Outreach Volunteering at Princeton

Princeton University — Princeton, NJ

Volunteers prepare scientific presentations across STEM topics (Molecular Biology, Chemistry, Neuroscience) to educate K-12 students who historically lack research university access.

Age Requirement: No minimum age limit

Requirement: Genuine interest in chosen scientific topic

Science Communication Visit Website
Hospital Care 04

Rutgers Cancer Institute Volunteering

RWJ Barnabas Health — New Brunswick, NJ

Serve as an integral team member providing patient services, escorting visitors, running comfort carts, and assisting with clinical/administrative support in NJ's premier cancer center.

Eligibility: Adults or College Students

Commitment: 9 months minimum (3-4 hr weekly shifts)

Clinical & Patient Support Visit Website
Museum & STEM 05

Liberty Science Center Volunteers

Liberty Science Center — Jersey City, NJ

Interact directly with guests while receiving customer service training. Benefits include flexible scheduling, LSC membership, cafe discounts, and professional development.

Age Requirement: Minimum 16 years old

Youth Commitment: 1 full day/week for 6+ months

Guest Services & Outreach Visit Website
